Sources and Timing

SOFR Overnight, 30-Day and 90-Day come from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, published around 8:00 AM ET each business day. Overnight is for the prior business day. 30-Day and 90-Day are backward-looking averages, the same series FRED lists as SOFR30DAYAVG and SOFR90DAYAVG. They are not CME Term SOFR, which is a forward-looking rate licensed by CME.

SONIA Overnight comes from the Bank of England, published at 9:00 AM London for the prior business day. 30-Day and 90-Day are calculated from the Bank of England SONIA index on an ACT/365 basis, using the same method the New York Fed uses for its SOFR averages.

Exchange Rates are ECB Reference Rates, set once a day around 4:00 PM Frankfurt time. They are Indicative Mid rates, not dealing rates.
